## Inlined in dist/index.js
Listed by the build from esbuild's metafile, in `build-inputs.json` in the repository (not shipped):
- Shaida's own code, MIT: `packages/shaida-proofs` (the link schema, PRD §5 verification core, reader hash, field
  constants), `packages/shaida-underwrite` (the decision engine, rules, codes and policy schema), the policy presets
  in `config/underwrite-policies.json`, and the income circuit's final verification key
  (`packages/shaida-proofs/keys/income_final_vkey.json`, sha256 2635a810…, from Shaida's phase-2 ceremony). The key
  is data generated by snarkjs from Shaida's income circuit, which uses circomlib (GPL-3.0) templates; it contains
  no circomlib source.
- `@noble/hashes` 1.8.0 (MIT, Copyright (c) 2022 Paul Miller): sha256 and bytesToHex, through `src/viem-shim.ts`,
  which reproduces two viem helpers (viem is MIT, Copyright (c) 2023-present weth, LLC). The build refuses to inline
  snarkjs, ffjavascript or any other GPL package (scripts/build.mjs).
